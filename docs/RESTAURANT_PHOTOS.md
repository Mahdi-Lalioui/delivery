# Taking photos for AWID

- Use daylight from a window. Turn off ceiling lights and flash. Tap the dish to focus and hold the phone steady.
- Photograph the portion you actually serve. Use a plain plate or a warm, neutral tabletop. Clear wrappers, clutter and people out of the frame.
- For a dish, shoot horizontally at about 45 degrees, or straight above a flat plate. Leave space around the food for the 4:3 crop and square thumbnails.
- For your cover, use a wide 16:9 composition. Keep the food near the centre so it survives the tighter Discovery crop. Upload logos separately as square images.
- Aim for 1,280 pixels across after cropping. Upload the original from your camera. Avoid screenshots, social filters, text overlays and heavy zoom.
- Check the preview. If the food still looks soft, too dark or oddly coloured, retake it with better light.

AWID applies a light warm grade, gentle contrast and mild sharpening to dish photos and restaurant covers. Logos and application evidence retain their colours. To update an existing live photo, upload its original again.
