# Tracking — round 1 flaws

Compared with Uber Eats and Deliveroo order tracking.

1. **Map legibility.** The route starts nowhere (no restaurant marker) and the destination is an
   anonymous dot instead of a home pin.
2. **Map surface.** The zellige pattern reads as wallpaper, not a city; a calm map is mostly flat
   with soft blocks and a few streets.
3. **Hierarchy.** Top apps make the arrival time the hero; here the status headline is big and the
   ETA is a small pill.
4. **Pickup order.** For pickup the code is what the customer needs at the counter; it should come
   right under the headline, before the timeline.
5. **Desktop.** The map card is shorter than the sheet and leaves a blank strip; it should match
   the sheet's height.
