# Tracking — round 3 flaws

Round 2 fixes landed: restaurant marker and home pin, route on a street, hero time on one line on
desktop, calm dark code card, status stepped down to a label heading.

1. **Progress.** The travelled and remaining parts of the route look the same; top apps fade the
   part already covered.
2. **Colour.** The destination halo is a muddy grey over the road; it should be a mint halo.
3. **Dead end.** With notifications blocked the card shows a negative message the customer cannot
   act on; it should disappear.
4. **Icon.** The restaurant marker's bag glyph reads like a lock at small size; a storefront shape
   is clearer.
5. **Layering.** The courier halo sits partly under the road outline; the marker must draw above
   the road and the route.

Memorable detail on this screen: the scooter marker glides to each new position and turns with its
heading while the route draws in.
