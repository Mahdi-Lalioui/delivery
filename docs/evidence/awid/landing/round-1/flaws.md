# Landing — round 1 flaws

1. **Crowding.** On phones a four-line intro and two stacked buttons fill the lower half of the hero.
2. **Competition.** The white preview button competes with the primary action.
3. **Typography.** The headline wraps with an orphan line.
4. **Legibility.** The photo credit is faint on bright sky.
5. **Continuity.** Nothing in the hero hints at the photo cuisines below; the slow zoom and
   crossfade are the memorable detail, so the first frame must read well on its own.
