# My orders — round 1 flaws

Real full-page route `/[locale]/dev/screens/orders`. Compared with Uber Eats and Deliveroo order
history.

1. **Verbosity.** The intro paragraph spends three lines explaining a list that explains itself.
2. **Placeholders.** Favourites without photos show huge single letters on flat mint.
3. **Actions.** Receipt and reorder sit on two separate rows; they belong in one action row.
4. **Crowding.** Status, code and date share one line and the date wraps unevenly.
5. **Rhythm.** The page is a column of loose cards with large gaps.
