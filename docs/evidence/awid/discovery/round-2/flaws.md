# Discovery — round 2 flaws

Round 1 fixes landed: short greeting, 2:1 collection cards, fixed-width cuisine chips with an
"All" icon tile, tighter section rhythm, staggered card entrance, search beside the headline on
desktop. Compared again with Wolt / Uber Eats:

1. **Typography.** Chip labels now break mid-word ("Sandwich-s", "Boulanger-ie"); chips need
   short names and normal word wrapping.
2. **Alignment (desktop).** The location pill floats alone above the headline while the search
   sits a row lower on the right; pill and search should share the right column.
3. **Hierarchy (phone).** The intro sentence still pushes the first restaurants down; on phones the
   headline alone is enough.
4. **Noise.** An "Open" badge on every card repeats the obvious; top apps only flag the exception
   (closed, opens at …). The badge also competes with the heart in the photo corners.
5. **Depth.** Rail cards and the full list look identical; the rails should feel like a lighter,
   swipeable layer (smaller cards) so the page has a clear foreground and background.
