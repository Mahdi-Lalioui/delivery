# Discovery — round 1 flaws

Compared with Wolt, Uber Eats and Deliveroo home screens.

1. **Hierarchy.** At 390 px the first screen shows no restaurant: a two-line display greeting
   and a location pill on its own row push all content below the fold.
2. **Typography.** The greeting is display-sized on desktop (64 px); top apps keep the home
   headline modest so photos and restaurants lead.
3. **Photo treatment.** Collection cards are tall (16:10), which magnifies soft source photos, and
   the caption block covers a third of the image.
4. **Rhythm.** Cuisine chips have uneven widths ("Cuisine algérienne" vs "Pizzas"), and "Tout"
   uses a dish photo so it reads as another cuisine instead of "all".
5. **Whitespace / structure.** 32 px gaps between every section feel loose; on desktop the search
   is a narrow pill under a huge headline instead of sitting with the location in one bar.
6. **Motion.** Nothing moves on load; cards pop in without the staggered entrance the brief asks.
