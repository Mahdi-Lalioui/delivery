# Discovery cards — round 1 flaws

New: a "Your favourites" rail from the hearts saved on this device, and a pointer tilt on cards
(mouse only, off under reduced motion). Compared with Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Wolt.

1. **Duplication.** The favourites rail looks identical to "Top rated" below it and here repeats
   the same two restaurants; it reads as a duplicate, not a personal shortcut.
2. **Rhythm.** Three rails in a row share one card size.
3. **Pattern.** Favourites should feel like quick access: smaller square tiles, as top apps
   show "order again".
4. **Scope.** The tilt applies to mouse pointers only, by design; phones are unaffected.
5. **Clutter.** The heart in the favourites rail is redundant: every tile there is a favourite.
