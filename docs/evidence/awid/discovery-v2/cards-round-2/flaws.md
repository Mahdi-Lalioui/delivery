# Discovery cards — round 2 flaws

Round 1 fixes landed: favourites are compact square tiles without the heart, so the page
alternates small and large rails.

1. **Truncation.** In the compact tiles long Latin names are cut on the left in Arabic
   ("ntika Bab El Oued"); two clamped lines read better than a hard cut.
2. **Height.** The rating sits on its own line under the name; acceptable, but the tile could
   be tighter.
3. **Rhythm.** Small rail then large rails now gives the page a clear pace.
4. **Hearts.** No repeated hearts in favourites.
5. **Tilt.** Desktop cards tilt a few degrees and gain a deeper shadow on hover.
