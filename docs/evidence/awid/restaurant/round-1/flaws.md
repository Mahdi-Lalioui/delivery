# Restaurant + dish sheet — round 1 flaws

Compared with Wolt and Uber Eats store pages.

1. **Layout.** "Most ordered" shows a single card beside empty space (it only listed dishes
   tagged popular).
2. **Structure (desktop).** The info panel stretches about 1 100 px wide for three short lines;
   it should be a compact card anchored to the cover's start edge.
3. **Noise.** An "Open" badge restates the default, as on the cards before.
4. **Dish sheet hierarchy.** The price only appears inside the Add button; top apps state it
   under the name with tags (popular, spicy, vegetarian).
5. **Motion.** Adding to the cart has no satisfying feedback beyond the bar appearing; this screen
   needs its memorable detail: the dish photo flying into the cart and the count springing.
