# Rating — round 1 flaws

Real full-page route `/[locale]/dev/screens/rating`. Compared with Uber Eats and Deliveroo rating.

1. **Warmth.** The hero is a generic plate icon; rating a meal should open on food.
2. **Meaning.** Stars spread edge to edge and nothing names the chosen score; top apps centre
   them and say "Excellent".
3. **Clutter.** The comment label carries its limit inline ("optional, 500 characters at most").
4. **Spacing.** On phones the submit button sits against the fixed tab bar.
5. **Weight.** Food and delivery get equal weight; food is the main question.
